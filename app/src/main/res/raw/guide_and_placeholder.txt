This directory is for your Persian voice files.
Please place your .mp3/wav files here and rename them as follows:

- Single digits: `n0.mp3` to `n9.mp3`
- 10 to 19: `n10.mp3` to `n19.mp3`
- Tens: `n20.mp3`, `n30.mp3` ... `n90.mp3`
- Teens and tens with connector (_): `n20_join.mp3`, `n30_join.mp3` ... `n90_join.mp3`
- Hundreds: `n100.mp3`, `n200.mp3` ... `n900.mp3`
- Hundreds with connector (_): `n100_join.mp3`, `n200_join.mp3` ... `n900_join.mp3`
- Thousand: `hezar.mp3`
